Privacy policy

Updated 27 November 2026

This policy describes the data handled by NutriScan’s website and Android app, maintained by Kunal Singh. For questions or a data request, contact kunal.singh.ug24@nsut.ac.in.

Information you provide

  • Account details. Email registration collects your name, email, password, date of birth, and an optional phone number. Passwords are stored as hashes.
  • Google sign-in. If you choose Google, NutriScan receives your Google account identifier, verified email address, and name to create or identify your NutriScan account. NutriScan does not receive your Google password and does not request access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar, or contacts.
  • Food and nutrition records. The service stores meals, serving details, nutrition estimates, dates and times, uploaded meal photos, saved foods, and nutrition alerts associated with your account.
  • Optional health profile. Details you choose to enter can include height, weight, activity level, allergies, and medical conditions.

How information is used

NutriScan uses this information to sign you in, keep your personal food library, display meal history and nutrition trends, calculate profile values such as BMI, and show nutrition alerts. These values and alerts are informational and use fixed reference values; they are not medical assessments.

Photo analysis and external services

When you request photo analysis, an image of your meal is sent to the configured image-analysis provider: Google Gemini or OpenAI. That provider returns food and nutrition estimates for you to review. NutriScan does not include your Google sign-in profile, account email, or health-profile fields in that analysis request. Information visible inside the photo is part of the image being processed, so choose photos accordingly.

When you save a photographed meal, the original uploaded image is stored with your account on NutriScan’s backend. Analysis requests and saving the original photo are separate operations.

NutriScan uses Vercel for website and API hosting and MongoDB for stored app records. These infrastructure services process data as needed to operate the service. Hosting and server systems may also process connection details and operational logs when handling requests or diagnosing errors. Google supplies optional sign-in services and fonts used by the app. Those services handle their own service data under their applicable terms.

See Google’s privacy policy and OpenAI’s privacy policy for information about those companies. Processing of API data is also governed by the applicable provider agreements and settings.

Google user data

Google account information is used for NutriScan account sign-in and identification. NutriScan’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage on your device

The app uses browser or WebView storage for your sign-in token, temporary sign-in state, and motion preference. Signing out removes the active local sign-in token. Clearing local browser storage does not delete data stored on the server. Camera and photo selection are initiated through the controls you choose to use.

Retention and your choices

Saved account, health-profile, meal, and photo records can remain stored until removed using supported app controls or handled through a data request. You can edit supported profile details and remove meal entries in the app. Removing an entry does not necessarily remove a saved-food copy or its original uploaded photo.

You can delete your account in Profile on the website or Android app. This removes your account, health profile, meal records, alerts, saved food library and stored photos from NutriScan’s active database. It does not delete information already held by outside providers under their own retention settings. For access, correction, export, or help with deletion, email kunal.singh.ug24@nsut.ac.in from your account email address. Account ownership may need to be verified before a request can be handled. Removing NutriScan’s access from your Google account stops that Google authorization; it does not itself delete your NutriScan records.

Temporary Google sign-in records expire after a short sign-in window and are eligible for automatic database cleanup. Infrastructure providers may keep operational records under their own retention settings.

Security

The hosted service uses HTTPS, password hashing for email accounts, and authenticated API routes for account records and stored meal photos. No internet service can promise complete security. Avoid including unnecessary personal information in meal photos or other submissions.

Changes and contact

This page may be updated as NutriScan changes; the date above identifies the current version. Contact kunal.singh.ug24@nsut.ac.in with questions about this policy or your data.
