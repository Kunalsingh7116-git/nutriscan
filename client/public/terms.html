NutriScan is a personal food-logging and nutrition-tracking service maintained by Kunal Singh. These terms explain the service’s intended use and limitations. Contact kunal.singh.ug24@nsut.ac.in with questions.

What NutriScan provides

The website and Android app let you record meals, request photo-based food estimates, review nutrition information, keep a food library, and view your daily nutrition history. Features depend on service and provider availability.

Review estimates before relying on them

Food identification, serving sizes, nutrition totals, BMI, and alerts can be incomplete or inaccurate. Review and correct estimates before saving them. Dashboard goals and alert thresholds are general reference values. NutriScan does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or personalized dietary recommendations. Consult a qualified professional for health decisions.

Your account and uploads

Use your own account and keep your sign-in information secure. Upload only images and information you are entitled to provide, and do not use the service to upload unlawful content, interfere with other accounts, or disrupt the service. Choose what you share with the photo-analysis provider and review the privacy policy before using those features.

Availability and records

The service and its external providers may be unavailable, rate limited, or changed over time. Keep another copy of information you cannot afford to lose. The app is not intended to be your only record for medical or clinical purposes.

Account and data requests

For questions, corrections, or a request to access or remove stored account information, contact kunal.singh.ug24@nsut.ac.in. The privacy policy explains the data stored by the service and the controls currently available.
